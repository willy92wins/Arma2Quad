# Notice and attribution

## The original material

The quad in this mod is Bohemia Interactive's ATV from **Arma 2: Operation Arrowhead**. Its
model (`.p3d`), textures (`.paa`) and materials (`.rvmat`) are the property of **Bohemia
Interactive a.s.**

They come from Bohemia's own Licensed Data Package for Operation Arrowhead,
`ALDP_A2OA_PBOs_DPL_APL_part2.zip`, listed at
<https://www.bohemia.net/community/licenses/licensed-data-packages> — they were not
extracted from the game. That package offers its data under DPL or APL, and Bohemia's
*Licensed Data Packages for DayZ* announcement (7 June 2019) makes these packages available
under the **DayZ Public License**, which is the license this mod uses.

A readme dated 2013 inside the package says "Arma only". It predates that announcement; the
2019 announcement is what puts this data under DPL for DayZ.

## Thanks

Thanks to **Bohemia Interactive** for Arma and DayZ, for the modding tools, and for
releasing these data packages to the community. This port exists because that material was
made available.

## What is new work here

`config.cpp`, `model.cfg` and everything under `scripts/` were written for this port: the
DayZ vehicle simulation setup, damage zones, lights, wheel items and their ruined variants,
sound sets and the Enforce Script classes. They are released under the same license as the
rest of the addon (DPL), so that the whole thing can be redistributed as one piece.
